New package: the design tokens shared by AuthOwl, Billow and Hitsend. It provides semantic OKLCH colours for light and dark with swappable brand accents, plus type, spacing, radius, elevation and motion. They ship as CSS variables (`tokens.css`), a Tailwind 4 theme (`theme.css`), shadcn/ui aliases (`shadcn.css`) and a typed export. Every approved text pairing passes WCAG AA for each brand and scheme.
